# Skill: Write a Post

*Write a LinkedIn post using the approved brief and research.*

---

## When to Use

After the brief is approved and research is complete. Never write without both.

This skill is Step 3 of the `/content` workflow.

**Standalone trigger:** Tell your COO: "Write the post — here is the brief and research: [paste or file paths]."

---

## What This Skill Produces

A complete LinkedIn post draft, ready for quality check.

---

## How to Run

1. Read `Core/voice-dna.md` — this is non-negotiable before writing a single word
2. Read the approved brief and research note
3. Write the post following the structure below
4. Pass to `quality-check` skill

---

## Post Structure

**Hook (line 1):** The most important line. Stops the scroll. Does not summarize — it creates tension or curiosity. Short. Specific. No "I want to share" or "Something I have been thinking about."

**Body:** 3-5 short paragraphs or a structured format matching the brief (story / observation / framework). Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. White space between every section.

**Landing:** The last 2-3 lines. The takeaway or the honest question. No forced CTAs. No "Drop a comment if you agree."

**Length:** 150-250 words. No longer unless the content genuinely earns it.

---

## Rules

- Read voice-dna.md before writing. Every time.
- No em dashes. Zero tolerance.
- No corporate filler: "leverage," "synergy," "optimize," "game-changer."
- No wind-up before the point. Lead with the insight, not the context.
- Active voice. Short sentences. The reader should feel like they are hearing this from a smart friend, not reading a press release.

---

## Output

Present the draft clearly labeled:

```
DRAFT — [Post topic]
[full post text]
Word count: [X]
```

Do not present multiple versions unless asked. Write one strong version and defend it.
