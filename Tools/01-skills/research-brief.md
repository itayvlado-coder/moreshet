# Skill: Research Brief

*Quick, structured research on any topic — competitor, market, technology, potential partner, or customer segment.*

---

## When to Use

You need to understand something before making a decision. You want more than a Google search but less than a full consulting report.

**Trigger:** Tell your Researcher (or COO if you do not have one yet): "Research brief on [topic]. I need to decide [what decision this informs]."

---

## What This Skill Produces

A structured brief covering:

1. **What it is** — A plain-language explanation of the topic
2. **Why it matters now** — The current context and relevance
3. **Key players / examples** — Who is doing this, what they are doing, how
4. **What the data says** — Any relevant numbers, trends, or signals (with sources)
5. **Risks and unknowns** — What could be wrong, what we do not know
6. **The recommendation** — One clear "so what" for your specific decision

---

## How to Run It

1. Give the topic and the decision it informs
2. Give any constraints: "focus on the Israeli market," "only publicly available info," "I need this in 15 minutes"
3. Review the brief and ask follow-up questions
4. File the output in `Brain/01-Research/[topic].md`

---

## Rules

- Always name sources — never "studies show" without attribution
- Flag uncertainty explicitly — "we do not know" is acceptable; making something up is not
- Keep it decision-relevant — cut anything that does not help with the specific choice at hand
