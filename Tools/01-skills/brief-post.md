# Skill: Brief a Post

*Create a structured brief for a new LinkedIn post before any writing begins.*

---

## When to Use

Before writing any post. A brief answers three questions upfront: what is the post about, who is it for, and what should the reader feel or do after reading it. Without a brief, you are writing blind.

This skill is Step 1 of the `/content` workflow.

**Standalone trigger:** Tell your COO: "Brief a post about [topic]."

---

## What This Skill Produces

A one-page brief containing:

1. **The topic** — what this post is specifically about (not a broad theme, a specific angle)
2. **The audience** — which segment of your ICP this is written for
3. **The insight** — the one non-obvious thing this post will say
4. **The format** — story / observation / framework / list / case study
5. **The hook** — a draft first line designed to stop the scroll
6. **The goal** — what you want the reader to think, feel, or do
7. **What to avoid** — anything off-brand, overdone, or off-message for this piece

---

## How to Run

1. Read `Core/voice-dna.md` and `Core/icp-profile.md`
2. Ask the CEO (if not already provided): "What is the topic or angle you want to explore?"
3. Draft the brief
4. Show it and ask: "Does this direction feel right before I research and write?"

Do not proceed to writing until the brief is approved.

---

## Output Format

```
POST BRIEF — [Topic]
Date: [date]

Topic: [specific angle, not broad theme]
Audience: [which ICP segment]
Core insight: [the one thing]
Format: [story / observation / framework / list]
Draft hook: [first line]
Goal: [what reader should think/feel/do]
Avoid: [what to stay away from]
```

---

## Notes

The brief is the most important step. A weak brief produces a generic post. A sharp brief writes itself.
