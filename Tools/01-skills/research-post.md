# Skill: Research a Post

*Gather the specific facts, examples, and angles needed to write a strong post.*

---

## When to Use

After the brief is approved, before writing begins. Good posts are grounded in specifics — real examples, honest observations, concrete details. This skill finds those details.

This skill is Step 2 of the `/content` workflow.

**Standalone trigger:** Tell your COO: "Research this post brief: [paste brief or file path]."

---

## What This Skill Produces

A research note containing:

1. **3-5 specific facts or data points** relevant to the topic (with sources named)
2. **2-3 real examples** — companies, people, or situations that illustrate the insight
3. **The strongest counterargument** — what someone who disagrees would say
4. **A personal angle** — something from the CEO's own experience that connects to this topic (ask if not obvious)
5. **What is overdone** — how this topic is usually covered, so we can do it differently

---

## How to Run

1. Read the approved brief
2. Read `Core/voice-dna.md` for angle and tone guidance
3. Research the topic — use what you know; flag when you are uncertain about a fact
4. Ask the CEO: "Do you have a personal story or experience that connects to this? Even a small one."
5. Produce the research note
6. Pass to `write-post` skill

---

## Rules

- Name every source. Never "studies show" without attribution.
- Flag uncertainty explicitly. If you are not sure a fact is accurate, say so.
- The personal angle from the CEO is worth more than any external data point. Always ask for it.

---

## Notes

Research is not about finding facts to fill space. It is about finding the two or three specific details that make a generic observation into a real, credible post.
