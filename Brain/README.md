# Brain — Your Knowledge Vault

Everything you want your AI to know that is not in Core or Memory goes here.

---

## Structure

```
Brain/
├── 04-INBOX/       ← Unsorted captures. Paste anything here first.
└── Templates/      ← Reusable formats for common knowledge tasks.
```

Add folders as your knowledge grows:
- `01-Research/` — market research, competitor notes, industry reading
- `02-Customers/` — interview notes, feedback, win/loss insights
- `03-Strategy/` — frameworks, positioning work, long-form thinking

---

## How to Use the INBOX

When you have an idea, a link, a note, or anything worth keeping — paste it into `Brain/04-INBOX/inbox.md`.

Tell your COO: "Process my inbox." They will sort and file everything.

---

## Templates

Pre-built formats for common knowledge capture tasks. Use them whenever you need to document something consistently.
