## What We Build

A documentary production company that takes raw army footage, event recordings, and interviews and edits them into cohesive multi-episode documentary series. We turn scattered military material — combat footage, interviews, event recordings — into a narrative arc that tells the story of a conflict.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Army units and military organizations accumulate huge volumes of raw footage — interviews, event recordings, combat and operational video — that sits unused because no one turns it into a coherent story. We solve that: we take the raw material and shape it into a real narrative series, so the story of the conflict actually gets told and reaches people, instead of sitting in an archive.

Exclusive access to source materials that no one else has.
