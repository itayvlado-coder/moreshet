# Feedback

*Customer feedback, content performance, and market signals. Separate from learning-log — this captures what the outside world tells us, not what the system figured out internally.*

---

## What Goes Here vs. Learning Log

| feedback.md | learning-log.md |
|-------------|-----------------|
| A customer said X about your product | You discovered a pattern in how customers respond |
| A LinkedIn post got 3x normal engagement | You learned that posts about X outperform posts about Y |
| A prospect raised objection Y in a call | You identified Y as the #1 objection and built a response |
| Raw market signal | Processed insight that should change behavior |

**The rule:** raw feedback lives here. When a pattern emerges from multiple feedback entries — something strong enough to change how you operate — your COO promotes it to `Memory/learning-log.md`.

---

## How to Add an Entry

Tell your COO: "Log this feedback: [what you heard / what happened]."

Or add it yourself:

```
## [Date] — [Source / Channel]
**Type:** [Customer / Content / Market / Other]
**What:** [What was said or what happened]
**Signal strength:** [One-off / Pattern forming / Clear pattern]
**Action needed?** [Yes — [what] / No / Watch]
```
